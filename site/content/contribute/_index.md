## Every Visitor Is an Investigator

You don't need a law degree. You don't need journalist credentials. You need the ability to read public records and follow documented evidence from one node to the next.

---

## What You Can Do Right Now

### 🔍 Walk the Graph
Open the [investigation graph](/graph/) and explore. Start with any node. Follow the edges. If you find a path between two nodes that isn't documented — that's a finding.

### 📋 Pull Public Records
Every actor page has an **Investigation Path** section with unchecked items. These are research targets nobody has hit yet. Pick one and run it:

| Database | What It Has | Cost |
|----------|-------------|------|
| [LARA MiBusiness Registry](https://mibusinessregistry.lara.state.mi.us/search/business) | Michigan entity registrations | Free |
| [Michigan Courts Case Search](https://courts.michigan.gov/case-search) | State court dockets | Free |
| [PACER](https://www.pacer.gov) | Federal court filings | $0.10/page ($3 cap/doc) |
| [TransparencyUSA](https://www.transparencyusa.org) | Campaign finance across all states | Free |
| [Michigan CFRS](https://cfrsearch.nictusa.com) | Michigan campaign finance filings | Free |
| [ICHAT](https://apps.michigan.gov/ichat) | Michigan criminal history | $10/search |
| [Michigan School Data](https://www.mischooldata.org) | Official school performance data | Free |

### 📝 File FOIA Requests
Michigan's Freedom of Information Act gives you the right to request public records from any government agency. Our [FOIA Toolkit](https://barry.primals.eco/investigate/foia-toolkit/) has templates ready to use.

### ⚠️ Flag Connections
See two nodes that should be connected? A person who appears in multiple investigations? A public record that contradicts what's on the graph?

### ✅ Verify Existing Evidence
Don't trust us. Trust the sources. Every claim on every evidence site can be independently verified:
- Clone the [git repository](https://git.primals.eco/publicRecord/detroit) and audit it
- Check every LARA ID, every case number, every campaign finance filing
- If something is wrong, [tell us](mailto:hello@clutchjustice.com) — truth matters more than being right

## Sourcing Standard

We don't accept tips without sources. Every claim must be tied to:

| Source Type | What Counts |
|------------|-------------|
| **Court records** | Case number, court, date, docket entry |
| **Public filings** | LARA entity ID, campaign finance filing, ARIN WHOIS |
| **FOIA responses** | Requesting party, responding agency, date, tracking number |
| **Published reporting** | Outlet, author, date, URL |
| **Statutes** | MCL or MCR citation |

If you can't source it, put it in an investigation checklist as a `- [ ]` item — a lead to pursue, not a finding.

---

## What We Will Never Ask For

- Your name (unless you choose to share it)
- Your location
- Your relationship to any case
- Money
- Access to private systems or databases

We work with public records because public records are permanent, verifiable, and belong to everyone.

## The Network Protects You

This platform is part of a cross-protection evidence network. Evidence is published across multiple nodes ([detroit.primals.eco](https://detroit.primals.eco), [barry.primals.eco](https://barry.primals.eco), [tuebor.primals.eco](https://tuebor.primals.eco)). Contributions verified and added to the graph become part of that network.

SLAPP one node — the others amplify. This is by design.
