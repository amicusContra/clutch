## How to Walk

1. **Hover** over any node to see its connections light up
2. **Click** a node to see its evidence page
3. Use **filters** to isolate money flows, political connections, judicial appointments, or family ties
4. Use **County** filter to focus on one investigation
5. **Collapse** groups to see the meta-structure

### What You're Looking At

This graph combines nodes from every active investigation. Each node is a person, entity, or institution documented from public records. Each edge represents a verifiable connection — money, board membership, family, employment, authorization, or corruption.

**The traveling salesman problem:** Visit every node. Find the shortest path that connects them all. In a corruption network, the shortest paths are the most dangerous — they reveal who controls what.
