## What This Is

**Clutch Justice** is an independent investigative journalism platform covering Michigan's courts, schools, and oversight systems.

This site — **clutch.primals.eco** — is the collaborative investigation layer. It takes the evidence published across the [primals.eco](https://primals.eco) network and makes it walkable. Patrons and visitors can navigate the investigation graph, explore connections, verify evidence, and help identify patterns that strengthen active cases.

│                    ACCOUNTABILITY LAYER                   │

**Evidence flows up.** Investigations feed evidence sites. Evidence sites feed the graph. The graph feeds accountability filings. Accountability filings create public record. Public record feeds the next investigation.

The cycle is the design.

## Who We Are

People who read court records, file FOIA requests, attend hearings, submit complaints, and publish what we find.

We are not attorneys. We are not parties to each other's cases. We are citizens exercising First Amendment rights to document institutional accountability — or its absence.

Some of us are investigative journalists. Some are parents. Some are both.

---

## Principles

1. **Every fact is sourced.** No claim without a citation. No finding without a document.
2. **Every correction is published.** If we get something wrong, we fix it publicly.
3. **No tracking.** No cookies. No identifying data. No surveillance of visitors.
4. **Cross-protection by design.** Evidence exists across multiple nodes. Suppress one — the others hold.
5. **Open source.** Clone the repositories. Verify everything. Trust the documents, not us.
